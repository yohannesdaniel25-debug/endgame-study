Move judgments come from a local Stockfish (ASM-JS build) search at the depth/time you choose above — a few hundred milliseconds per position is far shallower than the multi-second server-side analysis chess sites use, so treat classifications as a strong guide rather than gospel, especially near the "inaccuracy / good" boundary. The "hanging piece" and "brilliant" notes are simple heuristics, not full tactical-motif recognition. Everything runs locally in your browser; your games and history are stored only in this browser's local storage and are never sent anywhere except the initial fetch to Chess.com's public API.
